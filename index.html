Your ads × your first session

Keep the promise the ad made. Two of your ads, two first screens.

YouTube is where we see most of your ads, then Meta and Google. They ask different things: “どっちが好き？” on YouTube, “どの推しコーデが好き？” on Meta, and “初回無料のガチャがたくさん！” on Google.

Flow Builder can open a different first session by where the install came from, so the first screen repeats what the ad said, and each ad is judged on the first purchases it brings, not the installs.
